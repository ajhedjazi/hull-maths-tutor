How lessons work

Good maths teaching starts by listening.

One small gap can make maths feel hard. We work out what's getting in the way, then make the next step clear.

  1. Tell me what feels difficult.

    We'll look at the topics or questions you're finding hard.

  2. Build understanding, step by step.

    I'll explain it in a way that makes sense to you.

  3. Practise with a clear purpose.

    We'll focus on the next useful step, then build up to exam-style questions.
