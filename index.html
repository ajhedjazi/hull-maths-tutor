Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Add any other question to the enquiry form.

Do you only teach GCSE maths?

GCSE maths is the main focus. I also help post-16 resit students and students who need to rebuild earlier skills.

Do you offer online lessons?

Yes. One-to-one GCSE maths lessons are available online.

Do you offer in-person lessons in Hull?

Yes. The Maths MOT is in person. Weekly lessons may also be available in person, depending on your area and my timetable.

What happens in the £45 Maths MOT & Action Plan?

Your child works through a set of questions in a calm setting. I check their working, strengths, gaps and confidence. You get clear feedback and an action plan.

What if my child lacks confidence?

That is a common starting point. We slow things down and build confidence one step at a time.

Do you support neurodivergent learners?

Yes. Lessons can be shaped around the student’s needs, routine and pace.

Do you support Foundation and Higher GCSE?

Yes. Lessons match the student’s level, exam tier and goals.

Can tutoring align with school work?

Yes. With your permission, I can use school information to help lessons support current classwork.
