What is the Maths MOT?

A clear check of your child’s maths.

The Maths MOT is a one-hour, in-person assessment.

I look at how your child works, not just the final answer. This helps me find what they know, where they get stuck and why.

You get clear feedback and a simple action plan.

Who it helps

For students who work hard but still feel stuck.

It can help when your child does not know why they are losing marks.

  • They revise but still get stuck
  • They can do it in class but not in an exam
  • Long questions feel hard to start or finish
  • Low confidence gets in the way
  • They are working around Grades 3 to 5 or taking a resit

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Is this a normal tutoring lesson?

No. It is a one-hour assessment that finds strengths, gaps and the best next step.

Does my child need to prepare?

No. I need to see how they work now, so there is nothing to revise first.

Is it suitable for Foundation and Higher?

Yes. The MOT matches the student’s level, gaps and goals.

What happens after the MOT?

You get clear feedback and an action plan. You can then decide whether more tuition would help.

Do I have to commit to regular tutoring?

No. You do not have to book more lessons after the Maths MOT.

Is it available online or in person?

The Maths MOT is in person. Weekly tuition can also be arranged online.

Is it suitable for post-16 resit students?

Yes. I support post-16 students who are preparing for a resit.
