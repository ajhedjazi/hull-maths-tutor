Tuition enquiries

The enquiry form sends your name, email address, optional phone number, selected service and message to Netlify Forms so I can respond to your request. Netlify processes and stores submissions and applies spam protection. Netlify is configured to send enquiry notifications to my business inbox, mathstutoringhull@gmail.com. I keep those emails in that Gmail inbox and reply directly. No forwarding to another email account or external enquiry-management service is knowingly configured.

Name, email and message are required to send the form; a phone number is not. Please include only the information needed to explain your enquiry, and avoid sending sensitive information about a child in the initial message.

A successful website response redirects to the thank-you page. It does not confirm an appointment. Optional analytics consent is not required to send an enquiry.

When you ask me to arrange tuition with you, I use the information needed to take those steps before a contract. For general questions, relevant information a parent supplies about a learner, and a limited record of an unsuccessful enquiry, I rely on legitimate interests: answering requests for maths support and managing the resulting correspondence. I limit this use to information reasonably needed for those purposes, taking account of your expectations and rights, with particular care for children's information. A contract with a parent does not automatically cover every detail about a child. Please do not send medical reports or other sensitive records through the initial enquiry form.

Free Parent Guide downloads

The Parent Guide is a free, direct PDF download. You do not need to give your name, email address or maths tier. There is no guide form or Web3Forms submission, and future downloads collect no form details.

You can download or open the PDF without JavaScript or analytics consent. Downloading does not subscribe you to a mailing list or give marketing consent. The existing download-page address also remains available for older links.

Your browser still requests the page and PDF from the website's host. Ordinary network information needed to deliver those files may appear in hosting logs; direct download does not mean that no network data is processed. The guide and download pages do not load Google Analytics.

Earlier guide requests may remain in Web3Forms, notification emails or exports. They can include the name, email address and maths tier previously supplied, technical submission information and evidence of previous choices. Removing the form has not deleted those records. Any continued retention is limited to a specific, necessary purpose, such as finishing an outstanding request or keeping proportionate evidence of a previous choice, on the basis of legitimate interests in handling that request or demonstrating the choice. Historical records are reviewed separately under the retention process below. Downloading the guide now creates no guide-form record and gives no permission for marketing.

Optional measurement

Website analytics has one initially off choice in Privacy settings. Until you enable it, this site does not load Google's tag or send analytics events or consent pings to Google. Refusing analytics does not stop forms or PDF links.

If you enable Website analytics, Google Analytics 4 receives page visits, browser/device and network information, including the connection's IP address. The owner has confirmed that page-load views are on. Automatic scroll, outbound-click, site-search, form-interaction, video-engagement and file-download measurement, and browser-history page views, are off.

Separately, and only with your analytics consent, this site measures an enquiry returning a successful website response and clicks on telephone, WhatsApp and Maths MOT links. These custom events remain enabled even though automatic interaction measurement is off. They do not include your enquiry text or contact-form details. Guide, download, thank-you, privacy and legal-information pages do not load the Google tag. Direct guide downloads collect no form details or individual campaign-attribution fields.

The site does not intentionally send form contents, names, email addresses or phone numbers to analytics. Its configured page URL omits query strings and fragments, and its configured referrer is reduced to the originating website. Advertising storage, advertising user data, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled by the site's configuration.

Consent is the lawful basis for optional analytics. This site does not rely on a statistical-purpose exception. The owner has confirmed that Google Signals, user-provided data collection and granular location/device collection are off, and advertising personalisation is disabled in all regions. All four optional Google account-sharing settings are off: Google products and services, modelling contributions and business insights, technical support, and recommendations for the business. These account settings have not been independently reverified.

You can withdraw analytics consent using the same settings control. Turning it off stops new Google Analytics collection and clears this site's known Analytics cookies. This cannot recall in-flight data or erase records already processed by a provider; contact Amir about those records.

Privacy settings

Cookies and browser storage

  • hmt_analytics_v3: a first-party session cookie containing only allow or reject for website analytics. It has no visitor identifier or fixed expiry date. It is used only to remember and apply your privacy choice; browser session-restore settings can affect how long a session cookie remains. Earlier preference versions are ignored: a previous analytics or campaign choice cannot enable this version without a fresh opt-in.
  • _ga and _ga_FTB362ESXH: Analytics identifier cookies, allowed only after consent. This site's tag requests session-based cookies rather than a fixed multi-year expiry. Google's server-side retention is separate from these cookies.

The website does not use local storage or session storage to track you or save form contents. Fonts and images are hosted with the site rather than fetched from an external font service. Your browser may cache these public files normally. Starting a new browser session or clearing site cookies may require a new analytics choice. Old or unknown cookie values do not create a new permission to track you.

How long information is kept

The retention schedule for copies under the business's control is 12 calendar months from receipt for unsuccessful tuition enquiries, and 90 days from original receipt for historical Parent Guide requests without a continuing purpose. Future direct guide downloads create no form records to put on that schedule. A monthly review includes records due before the next review, removing unnecessary copies earlier where appropriate. This is a manual process, not automatic deletion; provider-held historical submissions may require a separate deletion request and follow-up.

Information needed for active tuition is kept while needed to provide and administer that service. Required accounting or tax records are retained for the applicable legal recordkeeping period, on the basis of that legal obligation. A specific complaint, rights request or legal claim can justify keeping relevant information longer where necessary, with a recorded reason and review date. These exceptions do not justify keeping every old message indefinitely.

Deleting a notification email does not delete a provider's stored submission. Netlify does not publish a fixed expiry for every live form record or recovery copy in the agreement reviewed; eligible submissions are deleted individually. Its termination provisions allow up to 90 days, with backup/log rotation and legal exceptions: that is not a 90-day live-enquiry policy. Web3Forms' current processing agreement describes a three-year submission lifetime, not our 90-day historical-record target. Earlier deletion of any historical records it still holds must be requested or carried out using the available account controls, and followed up.

Gmail messages moved to Trash remain recoverable for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted sooner. Eligible messages are removed from accessible Trash as part of the review. Google's published deletion information explains that system deletion generally takes around two months and encrypted backups can retain data for up to six months, with limited legal/security exceptions and possible delays. Provider recovery copies, logs and backups follow their own deletion cycles; this business cannot promise immediate removal from every backup.

The owner has confirmed that Google Analytics event-data and user-data retention are both set to two months, with reset on new activity off. New activity therefore does not restart the user-retention period. These controls apply to the user and event data covered by Google's retention settings; they do not set the lifetime of all aggregated reports or promise that every historical record has already been deleted. Google's server-side retention is separate from the session cookies described above.

Your choices and rights

Consent is the basis for optional analytics only. It does not supply the lawful basis for handling a tuition enquiry or historical guide records. You can refuse or withdraw analytics without losing access to the enquiry form or PDF.

Depending on the processing and circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to use of your personal information. You can withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact Amir using the details above to discuss a request.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. The business must consider your circumstances and the applicable rules; this is distinct from switching off analytics. Some information may need to be kept for a lawful obligation or a justified claim, so erasure is not an unconditional promise.

You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office. These rights and the ability to complain are not conditional on accepting analytics.
