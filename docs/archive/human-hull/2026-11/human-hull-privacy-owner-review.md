## Lawful-basis assessment for owner review

The [ICO contract guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/contract/) permits necessary steps requested by the prospective contracting individual. Proposed use: the enquirer's own details to respond about arranging their tuition contract. It does not automatically cover another person's data, general non-contractual questions or retention after negotiations end. Assess those separately under legitimate interests where justified. Do not use analytics consent as a substitute.
