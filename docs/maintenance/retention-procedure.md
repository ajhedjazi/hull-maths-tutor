# Retention and deletion - monthly operating procedure

20 November 2026. Operator: Amir Hedjazi, sole trader trading as Hull Maths Tutor. **Owner-approved on 20 November 2026, alongside the Privacy & Cookies Policy.** No reminder has been created and no customer record has been inspected or deleted by this task. New Parent Guide downloads create no form records; the guide procedure concerns historical requests only.

## Dates and exceptions

| Record | Limit for copies controlled by the business | Calculate from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsuccessful tuition enquiry, notifications and related correspondence | 12 calendar months | Original receipt; use the last day of the month if the anniversary date does not exist |
| Historical Parent Guide request with no continuing purpose | 90 calendar days | Original request, not a later email, export or internal note |
| Active tuition records | While needed to provide/administer tuition | Keep only relevant information; review when tuition ends |
| Necessary accounting/tax, complaint, rights-request or legal-claim records | Applicable legal period or justified continuing need | Record purpose, applicable obligation where relevant, limited scope and next review date |

A genuine new enquiry has its own date. Do not restart old records' clocks by exporting, forwarding or making notes. Delete sooner when no longer needed. Do not classify every old guide/enquiry email as an active student record. Historical consent evidence, if needed to show a past choice, is a scoped exception with its own reason and review date; it does not permit new marketing. This is not a new indefinite archive.

## One monthly appointment

Owner action: put a recurring reminder on the **first working day of each month**, with the first review before public launch. Check records already due and those due **before the next appointment**, deleting unnecessary copies early so a monthly review does not routinely add a month. If an exception genuinely must last until a precise date, set a separate reminder. Record and remedy missed reviews promptly.

1. Identify active tuition, financial records and justified holds first. Work only on eligible records; do not delete a form, account or whole financial archive.
2. **Netlify:** in Forms > enquiry, select and delete only eligible individual submissions, checking relevant Spam entries too. Preserve the `enquiry` form. [Official submission controls](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/submissions/). A deploy-artifact expiry is not form retention.
3. **Gmail:** remove matching notifications, replies and unnecessary attachments from the configured Gmail Inbox/All Mail, Sent and accessible Trash, checking any synced local mail copies. The owner confirms direct replies from this inbox and no knowingly configured forwarding/external enquiry manager; do not invent extra destinations. Archiving alone is not deletion. Gmail normally keeps Trash for 30 days; use permanent deletion for eligible messages in the review. [Gmail deletion instructions](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7401?hl=en).
4. **Historical Web3Forms:** once, establish whether historical records still exist and whether the account offers individual deletion; if they do, use that control or request deletion through the provider's published privacy/support contact. Specify only the necessary record identifiers privately. Do not export an entire history just to identify a few records. Record requests as pending until confirmed, and follow up at the next review. Do not claim that Gmail deletion purges Web3Forms. Its [privacy policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy) and [DPA](https://web3forms.com/dpa) do not provide an automatic 90-day account purge. Historical account contents/plan and earlier deletion have not been inspected here. If there are no records in that location, record that and stop checking the retired integration unnecessarily.
5. **Exports/copies:** check known Downloads, local/cloud files, spreadsheets, attachments, accessible recycle bins and version histories; remove eligible copies/rows. Do not create new bulk copies to perform deletion. Keep any necessary export private, out of Git/the website, with the original due date.
6. Make the short private log below; note outstanding provider actions and scoped exceptions. Verify completion of a requested action before marking it complete. Keep no names or message bodies in this housekeeping log; review the log's own continuing need at each review.

## Provider copies and limits

Netlify's [DPA](https://www.netlify.com/pdf/netlify-dpa.pdf/) distinguishes termination deletion from backup/log rotation; it does not specify a fixed live-form expiry. Do not cancel the account to meet this procedure. Web3Forms' DPA distinguishes submission lifetime, termination and residual backups; ask about the particular historical records, not an invented automatic purge. Account visibility is not proof of physical erasure.

Google's [deletion explanation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention?hl=en-GB) describes an approximately two-month process and backups up to six months, with exceptions/delays. These are provider limits, not something this monthly procedure can accelerate or certify. Netlify has not published a universal backup rotation duration in the DPA reviewed. Ask the provider only if a specific outstanding request needs a recovery-copy answer; an unknown universal number does not prevent an accurate notice based on retention criteria. If restoring a business backup, reapply recorded deletion decisions before returning obsolete records to routine use.

The owner-confirmed GA4 two-month event/user retention, reset off, is separate from these business records and session cookies. No account change or historical deletion has been performed here.

Handle access, correction, erasure, objection and withdrawal requests when received, without waiting for the monthly review. Verify identity proportionately, find the relevant copies/holds and respond within applicable deadlines. Analytics withdrawal stops new optional collection; it cannot recall in-flight requests or automatically erase provider history.
