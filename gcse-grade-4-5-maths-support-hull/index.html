Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Every plan is personal. No grade is guaranteed.

Is this only for students currently at Grade 3?

No. It is for students working around Grades 3, 4 and 5.

Can you guarantee a Grade 4 or 5?

No. I can help with gaps and exam skills, but I cannot promise a grade.

What if my child knows topics in class but freezes in exams?

We look at method choice, confidence, wording and timing as well as the maths.

Is this suitable for Foundation and Higher?

Yes. The plan matches the student’s level, gaps and goals.

Should we start with the Maths MOT?

Yes, if you are not sure why marks are being lost. It gives you a clear place to start.
